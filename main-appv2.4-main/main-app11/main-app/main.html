Look & Feel

Appearance

The background drifts away from your cursor while the icons and widgets lean gently toward it, giving the window a subtle sense of depth. Text stays pixel-sharp. Off by default.

Background — animated scenes, your own media, and effects

Pick a built-in animated scene, design your own with My Scene, or add your own image, GIF, or looping video. Your files are copied into the app's data folder, so they keep working even if the original moves. On the Transparent background, the Blur, Brightness, and Saturation sliders apply to a live view of whatever is really behind the window — your desktop, games, other apps — for a true frosted-glass look with rounded corners. Heads-up: while those effects are on, the app hides itself from screenshots and screen recordings (that's what makes the live view possible).

My Scene — pick your colors

Your three colors become an animated scene — soft light pools plus, optionally, big drifting blobs. Changes apply live.

Media fit

Accent theme

Tints the app's glow — the window halo, performance bars, sliders, toggles, music progress, and lyrics — to match. Auto follows whichever background is active (for your own media it samples the picture's dominant color, and on Transparent it watches the screen behind the window and keeps re-tinting live), or pick any color with the eyedropper swatch.

Text color

Recolors the app's text — headings, labels, and values keep their light-to-dim hierarchy in your chosen tint. Auto follows the accent theme, and any color you pick is automatically brightened enough to stay readable. Colored buttons (like Spotify's green) are left alone.
