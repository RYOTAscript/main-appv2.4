Pair with your PC

Your music player and lyrics from main, on this phone. From anywhere.

  1. On your PC, open main and go to the Widget Library.
  2. Open Phone Remote, turn it on and tap Pair a phone.
  3. Point this phone’s camera at the code, and tap the link.

Pairing is end-to-end encrypted. Nobody in between — not even main’s relay — can see what you play.
